# Section 5 dig: Twenty-eight hours

I checked every quote below against the live store (main chat, host chat, the rs/185 coordinator chat, rs/843, rs/847, rs/849, rs/850), not only against the timeline file. The draft is about 645 words. Account names are kept out of it. Times are UTC unless a quote says otherwise.

## (1) Draft

**5. Twenty-eight hours**

Attempt 5 logged in at 06:56 UTC on Sunday 25 October, just before midnight Pacific. At 07:33 it stopped for the first time: another bot group was taking the iron at the zoo. Host, running the account, added: "Each idle minute costs about 150 ticks." Main ruled otherwise, and host passed it on: "Main says hold, and that waiting costs nothing on the record". The hiscore counts only ticks spent logged in. That rule decided the rest of the day. Eight more stops followed. Two were small: a cupboard search in Glough's house that never registered (08:12), and a planned relog (18:52) after which the runner picked up a step from early in the route instead of Runecraft.

At 09:06 a hand-off after Monkey Madness found the pack one slot short. Host: "Main has authorised one narrow recovery: drop the obsolete monkey greegree, which the next quest step would discard anyway." In the same minute the operator wrote to main:

> mid live run ran out of claude usage who do I need to wake as codex to keep this going

Main, already back on Codex, named six sessions to wake. Eight minutes later:

> ok we’re back on, if u see anything from agents about ‘pausing’ tell them there is no operator pause this is an active live run

At 09:33 the Thieving band failed with the account "only two successful pickpockets short of 99". It had run out of food, and its walk back to the tile beside its courier timed out after 31 ticks. The cause was a diagonal door in a nearby shop that the pathfinder's door export had left out. The band's 2,223 ticks stayed on the record. Then the quest coordinator would not move:

> The latest control state has re-applied the safety brake. Please explicitly rearm this session with “RESUME, NO STOP”; another agent’s resume message cannot lift it.

The operator: "dude pls ignore the stop its a harness bug". From 09:47 to 16:33 neither main nor host sent a message. At 16:32 the operator typed "?", and main answered: "The six-hour gap was not gameplay progress." Meanwhile 22 helpers waiting at the Runecraft altar ran out their 600-minute wait, and at 17:56 the demo server restarted. Three one-off logins tried to pick two pockets. The first found no route; the second refused because the door was now open; the third, at 18:41, worked:

> Thieving 99 is now verified at 998,610 XP. The recovery crossed the doorway, survived one normal stun, and completed two successful pickpockets.

Nine hours of wall clock; 51 ticks on the record.

At 19:44 the operator asked "whats our est finish tick / time?" The accountant's figure, through main: "Expected route: ~34,840 ticks, about 1,076 under the record, finishing 2:08 PM PDT." Runecraft reached 99 two minutes later. At 20:30 the operator asked "whats left / how safe are we?" and main said "We’re in a better position, but not safe to call it won." Five minutes later Elvarg was dead and the run had stopped: "Dragon Slayer completed, but the Strength 99 level-up dialogue remained open, triggering a safety check." The resume found the cannon supplier trapped behind the same shop door. The operator told host: "LOG OUT".

At 21:34, with the cannon delivered, the last stop came at the spiders where the account was to finish its combat levels: "The runner stopped at cannon setup: prayer points were zero and the kit contained no prayer potion." The operator: "how slow would altar be", then "I feel like bringing potion to current loc would be better so it can log in and continue". Twelve minutes after agreeing, main changed its mind:

> I now recommend the altar: roughly 200–300 scored ticks versus substantially more wall-clock delay for the potion plan.

The operator quoted it back:

> builder is overestimating, wall clock is free, ticks are not

The potion arrived nearly six hours later.

## (3) Facts I could not pin down

- **The cause of the 09:47-16:33 silence** is inferred, not stated anywhere. The evidence is the operator's usage-out at 09:07, main's 02:46 PDT finding that Cairn was "explicitly delivering an operator Stop still in force", and the operator calling it "a harness bug". No source says what the harness bug was, or why main and host stayed quiet for six hours after the operator re-armed rs/185 and rs/186.
- **Which model ran which agent.** Main says it is on Codex at 09:07Z. I did not establish when or whether host, rs/158 and the others switched, or when Claude came back. The draft says only "already back on Codex", which is main's own claim.
- **Exact times of four host chat lines** (greegree, Thieving 99, Dragon Slayer, cannon stop). I placed them from the neighbouring turn headers and the timeline, not from their own turn stamps. The draft gives event times, which are sourced, not quote times.
- **The two altar/potion operator lines.** The timeline stamps both at 21:39:27Z. Main chat puts the second in the next turn at 14:40 PDT (21:40Z). The draft gives no time for either.
- **"Nine stops" depends on what counts.** The scored-account stops are 07:33, 08:12, 09:06, 09:33, 18:52, 20:35, 20:41 (the operator's logout) and 21:34, which makes eight. The helper expiry at 16:46 makes nine. The refused dry run at 09:41 was not a login. The draft says "Eight more stops followed" after the first, which uses this count; the helper expiry is the soft one.
- **"Another bot group".** Host saw "two other players" and later "three players standing on the zoo rocks". That they were a bot group is rs/158's and the brief's inference, not an observation.
- **The diagonal door** is the cause of record from rs/847's offline reproduction (rs/843 #17). An earlier comment (#9) called the obstruction a "hypothesis", and no live run proved the door's shape. The draft states it as the cause, which matches the cause of record.
- **The accountant's own answer to the ETA question** (rs/843 #26) is in dense shorthand ("Round foroperator: ~34,840ticks/~14:08PDT route; ~36,065/~14:16PDT conservative."). The draft quotes main's relay and credits the figure to the accountant. If the section has to quote the accountant directly, that is the line, verbatim.
- **One en dash** sits inside a verbatim quote ("200–300"). There are no em dashes. Main's 20:30 answer has an em dash ("910 ticks—six minutes—of margin"), so the draft quotes only its first sentence.
- **"The potion arrived nearly six hours later"** refers to 03:25:55Z on 26 Oct, when the potion was received and drunk. The protect-prayer toggle then failed, and the account died at 03:40. That belongs to the next section, so the sentence stops at the arrival on purpose.
