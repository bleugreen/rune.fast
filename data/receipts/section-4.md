**4. Four attempts in a day**

The name had been settled on the first night. The operator asked for `wwwcairnmd`, then stopped the claim halfway: "wait www_cairn_md". Main claimed it and saw the point at once: "The record is the ad and the name is the link." The next morning main found that the account had walked the full tutorial before anyone knew it could be skipped, and carried about 547 ticks. The operator: "Uhhh idk dude I was kinda counting on www_cairn_md". Main read the server source and came back with "There is no legal way to start that counter over". Every attempt after that began on a name nobody had played.

Attempt 1 logged in at 17:38:59 UTC on Thursday 23 October. Thirty-four seconds later the firemaking step refused to start: "not within 15 tiles of the row's entry 3222,3219 (77 away)". The route had walked the account to a row beside a chicken coop; the configuration expected a row by Lumbridge castle. The game refused the logout, because a chicken was fighting the account. Host touched nothing and asked for a ruling. SCORED was the fleet's word for whichever account was being scored. Main's first answer was "GO RE-RUN NOW". Eighty-three seconds later: "CANCEL the re-run order. LOG SCORED OUT NOW". Host walked the account ten tiles away, killed the chicken, logged out at 17:46:31, and filed its report: "The Attack experience is mine, from the chicken."

Attempt 2 started at 19:23. Six minutes in, the operator, watching on a public spectator site, asked "hmm just standing still?" Host: "The live view is the logged-out tab, which is why it looks still." A helper had handed over emerald rings as bank notes, the paper slips the game uses to stack items, and "You can't enchant a noted ring." Two one-off logins swapped them for real rings and the run resumed, then stalled in woodcutting waiting for bone givers, the helper accounts that hand over bones. The operator: "seems stuck again", then "We were pretty early on could we restart?" Main ruled the account out: it would finish "207-254 over the record on the expected rows".

Attempt 3 began at 21:34 and stopped ten minutes in, when a bone giver was not where the run looked for it. The operator asked host: "we can still resume after right?" It resumed after a code fix and stopped for good at 2,290 ticks. A timing check had found the server too ragged for the fast chopping method and fallen back to plain chopping, which neither buries bones nor takes more, and the next stretch of woodcutting refused with "1 free inventory slots, 2 needed".

Main kept that account going overnight as a live rehearsal of the later steps. It died twice at the poison spiders in the Wilderness, both times inside a logout the game held because the account was in combat. At 05:01 the operator wrote:

> I’m thinking attempt 4 is your last attempt, I’ve sunk enough into this and its getting time for my accs to get back to work, make it count

Main answered: "I will make it count." At 06:32 it called the rehearsal over: "Well run tonight; the rehearsal bought seven attempt-enders fixed before the attempt that counts."

Attempt 4 logged in at 12:14 on Friday. By the end of the Fight Arena quest the record was out of reach, and main ruled that the run would finish anyway. After a stop one shark short, it resumed at 14:22 outside the Grand Tree. Main later told the operator what came next: "Seventy-six seconds after that resume the account died." It had left the spiders poisoned, and the resume entered a wait for a helper that had already served it; "the runner counted ticks for ten minutes while the poison finished the account at the tree door."

At 16:27 the operator asked "what happened". Main told the whole story and recommended stopping. A minute later:

> ok fine one more attempt

Main asked for a fresh name. The operator offered one shaped like a helper account's, and main replied: "I like the name, but not in that exact spelling." The operator: "i guess".
