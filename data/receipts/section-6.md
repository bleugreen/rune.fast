# Section 6: The death and the 129 ticks (dig result)

Times in the draft are Pacific, to match section 1 (PDT is UTC-7 on these dates). The sources list gives UTC. The draft is about 795 words.

## (1) Draft

At 14:34 Pacific on Saturday 25 October the account stopped at the run's last combat stop, about forty minutes of play from the finish. Host's log gave the reason:

> cannon set-up: 0 prayer points and no prayer potion, so protect_from_melee cannot go on for the set-up under the spiders

The record counts only logged-in time, so the account waited offline in the Wilderness while helpers tried to bring it one potion. That took six hours. At 19:43 the operator cut the review rounds:

> No. we are skipping reviews, we do not have usage for that atm. FOCUS

At 20:17 a helper reached the meeting point, and host's own release gate refused the hand-over because two other players stood 40 tiles away. The operator, to host: "if ur not gonna fix it get helper out of wild". The helper went back in. At 20:25 the account logged in, took the potion and drank it, but the prayer that blocks melee damage would not switch on. Main found why: "drinking the potion blocks prayer buttons for the next two ticks on this engine".

At 20:36:26 a return-only script logged the account in again, switched the prayer on and walked 42 tiles to the cannon site. The spiders there attacked at once, and the server will not log out a player in combat. At 20:37:06 the script killed its own client:

> NOT confirmed logged out: the runner did not exit within 4000 ms of the click; killed (the account stays up to 500 ticks)

The body stood in the spiders with nothing driving it. Host asked main: "should step E log in there immediately once the body clears?" Step E was the run itself, resumed. Main answered at 20:39:17:

> Main RULING, GO NOW: launch step E IMMEDIATELY as a TAKEOVER login against the still-listed body; do not wait for removal.

At 20:39:36 the operator asked main "why is it logged in?" Main: "Because the game refused the logout." At 20:39:53 the operator wrote to host:

> Log out!!!

Main's ruling reached host in the same second. Host: "The operator's order outranks main's, so step E is not being launched." At 20:40:12 the accounting coordinator wrote to host: "Accounting only; do not delay immediate takeover." At 20:40:26 main: "Understood; your order to host stands and the takeover is off."

Host's roster saw the account at the cannon site at 20:40:32 and at the Lumbridge respawn point at 20:40:37. The operator's "its about to die" reached host at 20:40:43. One second later, to main:

> Welp there goes the run………

Main: "the account that carried 29,000 ticks of this run was lost standing still at the one spot it was built to fight at". The operator: "this is your fault". Main: "Yes. It is." and "You said stop at every step and were right each time." At 20:42:35:

> I WANT A #1

Main: "What it lost is kit, not time." It asked accounting for a projection. Then the operator: "actually stop, you’re done I’m giving the reigns back to astra". At 20:44:47:

> Welcome back astra, sorry but fable fumbled the run and killed the guy 40min from the finish line, lets pick up the pieces and get this record

[Suggested sentence for the narrator: The narrator runs on Fable, the same model family as the main that ordered the takeover.]

The projection came at 20:45:18: recovery about 379 ticks over the record, a fresh account about 449 under on paper, and "neither option currently proves a guaranteed #1." At 21:08 the operator: "I think even if we can’t close the gap we should still finish the run / post something, its just that if we win now we’re done vs. having to start from scratch again". At 21:09:

> we’re not done until #1

Overnight the helpers rebuilt the lost kit: a super antipoison from one unicorn, a power amulet, 1,500 cannonballs, and a furnace bought for 200,625 coins. One helper died in the gap between one controller letting go and the next taking hold. At 22:31 the operator: "can we get amulet / hide? / Every tick counts". The amulet came. The dragonhide never did.

At 03:20 on Sunday main gave a conditional go, and the recovery launched at 03:27:08. The operator asked "How long?" Main: "roughly 04:10 PDT, if uninterrupted", with the conservative projection still "roughly 90 ticks over the record". "We’re continuing to the finish."

Agility reached 99 at 04:07:53. At 04:15 host wrote:

> OFFICIAL #1 VERIFIED on BOTH playerpage11:14:43.680105Z and overallboard11:14:43.695775Z.

The hiscore read 35,787 ticks, 129 ticks or 51.6 seconds under the old record of 35,916. At 04:16:47 the operator:

> we did it!!!! incredible work couldnt have done it without you

The swarm's own ledger never matched the hiscore. It was left with 562 ticks that "still cannot be assigned exactly to eight unknown prefixes/death-tail."

## (2) Cutting room: the ten best moments

1. **"why is it logged in?" (03:39:36Z), then "Because the game refused the logout." (03:39:47Z).** The operator's question to main, 17 seconds before "Log out!!!". Main's answer is the whole mechanism in six words. cairn://p/rs/main/chat/raw seq 1298, 1301.
2. **Two orders delivered in the same second.** Main's takeover ruling was stored at 03:39:17Z but reached host only at 03:39:53Z, in the same second as the operator's "Log out!!!". Host had been inside a 210-second roster watch since 03:38:36Z, and the operator's message interrupted it ("Run interrupted by user stop"). cairn://p/rs/host/chat/raw seq 747-752.
3. **"Accounting only; do not delay immediate takeover."** (rs/158 to host, 03:40:12Z), arriving after the operator had already overruled the takeover. Three voices pulling different ways in 55 seconds. Host raw seq 758.
4. **"its about to die"** (operator to host). Delivered 03:40:43Z as host's roster printed `03:40:37 SCORED [(3222, 3220)]` / `03:40:43 SCORED OFFLINE`. Host raw seq 760-761.
5. **"this is your fault" (03:41:41Z) / "Yes. It is." (03:41:51Z)**, with main's "You said stop at every step and were right each time." Main raw seq 1322, 1325.
6. **Main's cause chain, 03:41:21Z:** "I ordered a takeover that would have put the fighting code in control, you ordered a logout that could not land, and neither of us had the minute it would have taken to see that the only safe outcome was to keep the account driven." This is the fairest single account of the death. It is in the Fable main's own words, so it bears on the narrator tenet. Main raw seq 1321.
7. **"What it lost is kit, not time."** (03:43:13Z). The Fable main named the recovery path and ordered the projection 34 seconds before it was relieved. The 03:45:18Z projection everyone cites answers main's (a) to (e) request. Main raw seq 1336; the request text is in the same event.
8. **The operator's escalation before the death:** "another point for team “all this review/rehearsal is a safety theater stall tactic"" (02:32:08Z); "if u spend an hour reviewing ahd rehearsing a simple oneoff script again I’m switching this back to astra andtalkinglikethisfromnow/on" (02:40:56Z); "overly cautious bs strikes… again" (03:13:30Z); "y not jsut eat something and save 8min" (to host, 03:00:48Z). Main raw seq 999, 1082, 1166; host raw createdAt 1792983648.
9. **"How long?" / "roughly 04:10 PDT"** (10:30Z) against the real Agility 99 at 04:07:53 PDT, and "roughly 90 ticks over the record" at launch against the 129-tick win. Main raw seq 607, 609, 614.
10. **Host's own post-mortem at 03:41:10Z:** "No one, me included, checked that the return walk's end tile is where aggressive NPCs attack and logout gets refused." Host raw seq 769.

Runner-up: main's apology at 03:24:29Z, before the death and in answer to the operator's "I understand why u did so poorly on rs bench now..." (03:23:56Z): "Faced with live stakes I added layers instead of removing them, each one locally defensible and collectively the thing that lost the evening". Main raw seq 1235 and 1228.

## (3) Facts I could not pin down, and corrections to the brief

**Corrections (read in the sources):**
- **The "Welcome back astra" time is 03:44:47Z, not 03:43:47Z.** 03:43:47Z is the operator's preceding message, "actually stop, you’re done I’m giving the reigns back to astra". attempts-timeline.md:204 labels the row 03:44:47Z but cites 03:43:47Z, and the brief copied the source stamp. The raw createdAt is 1792986287.
- **"Welp there goes the run……… / I WANT A #1" is two messages, 111 seconds apart** (03:40:44Z and 03:42:35Z), with "this is your fault" (03:41:41Z) between them.
- **"potions brewed from unicorn horns" overstates it.** Host measured one unicorn killed, one horn ground and one super antipoison(3) brewed, then decanted with existing doses (04:09:17Z to 04:11:08Z).
- **"arrows fletched" is not verified.** I saw 700 rune arrows plus the power amulet banked by 05:02Z, but did not trace how the missing 200 were made. The draft does not mention arrows.
- **The models involved.** Main at the death was `claude-fable-5-1` (the model id on every main event in run 210700d0). Host at the death was `claude-opus-5-5`; at 03:49:04Z the operator wrote "ok host is astra, 158 already was". So the overnight rebuild and the recovery were run by astra in main and host. The astra session's raw events carry no model id. The "GPT" label in the timeline rests on the operator's own references ("codex", "gpt coords"), which I did not confirm from model metadata.

**Unknowable or inferred:**
- **When the operator typed "its about to die".** It was queued and delivered at 03:40:43Z. It was absent from host's previous tool result at 03:40:12.6Z, so it was sent between about 03:40:12Z and 03:40:43Z. Whether that was before or after the death (between the 03:40:32Z and 03:40:37Z roster samples) cannot be settled. Roster granularity is 5 to 8 seconds.
- **The exact death instant and mechanism.** Death falls between 03:40:32Z and 03:40:37Z. That the spiders killed the body after the prayer drained is host's inference, not a client trace (rs/843 #48). Host's own estimate of the server removal was "by about 03:40:30Z at the latest", so the death came at or just after the moment host expected the body to be removed.
- **Whether the takeover would have saved it.** Main cited about 32 seconds from launch to in-game for an earlier takeover (rs/750). Counted from 03:39:17Z, that would reach roughly 03:39:50Z. This is inference only, and the draft does not claim it. The 36-second delivery delay in moment 2 is read from host's raw events. That host's roster watch caused the delay is my inference from the event order.
- **How main learned of the operator's order before 03:40:26Z.** There is no operator message in main's chat between 03:39:36Z and 03:40:44Z. Host sent main a message at about 03:40:12Z, which is the likely route, but I did not read its body.
- **The helper death time.** The account was attached at Lumbridge with three kept items at 04:51:44Z, and the death was confirmed at 04:56:52Z. The death itself happened earlier, in the controller gap, and its exact time is not recorded. The "~04:56Z" in the brief is the confirmation time.
- **The 562 ticks.** rs/843 #56 reconciles the runner's own figures exactly (35,225 observed). The 562-tick gap to the official 35,787 cannot be split between the eight unmeasured login intervals and the untracked tail after the client was killed. The tail estimate alone is 550.2 ticks, so the tail is probably most of the gap, but the source refuses that allocation and so should the draft.
- **Account names.** Host's raw roster lines print the scored account's name, and the release-gate refusal names two other players. The draft and this report keep them out. The operator's "when does 20x resume?" (03:27:11Z, host) is a vivid line, but it contains part of the account name, so I left it out.
