Fonts served by rune.fast

Rune Serif (RuneSerif-Roman.var.woff2, RuneSerif-Italic.var.woff2)
  A Modified Version of Source Serif 4 (version 4.005, variable, wght 200-900, opsz 8-60)
  by Frank Griesshammer for Adobe, https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif.
  Modification: subset to Latin-1 plus general punctuation, arrows and math signs,
  with every OpenType layout feature kept (onum, lnum, tnum, pnum, smcp, c2sc, frac,
  sups, case, liga, zero). Renamed from Source Serif 4 to Rune Serif because the
  original reserves the font name 'Source'. Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1,
  see SourceSerif4-LICENSE.md. Thanks to Adobe and the Source Serif contributors.

IBM Plex Sans (IBMPlexSans-{Regular,Medium,SemiBold}-Latin1.woff2)
  The unmodified Latin1 split files from IBM Plex Sans 1.1.0 as published by IBM,
  https://github.com/IBM/plex. Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1, see
  IBMPlexSans-LICENSE.txt.
